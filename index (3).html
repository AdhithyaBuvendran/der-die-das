The rules behind the articles

die
  • -ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft (always)
  • -ion, -tät, -ik, -ei, -ie, -ur
  • -in for women die Lehrerin
  • most words in -e die Tasse
  • female people die Mutter
der
  • male people der Vater
  • days, months, seasons, times of day (not die Nacht)
  • -er people and devices der Lehrer, der Computer
  • -ling, -ist, -ismus
  • weather der Regen, der Schnee
das
  • verbs used as nouns das Duschen
  • -chen, -lein (always)
  • -ment, -um, and -o / -i / -y loanwords das Auto
  • many Ge- words das Gemüse, das Geld
  • das Kind, for any child
die
  • every plural, whatever the singular das Kind → die Kinder
  • compounds: the last word decides das Haus → das Krankenhaus
  • no rule fits? learn noun and article as one word
Vocabulary: Deutsch lernen!, Der Sächsische Ausländerbeauftragte, Dresden (9th edition). The rules are tendencies with exceptions; the quiz flags the exceptions when you meet them.
